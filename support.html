To help us resolve your issue faster, please include the following in your email:

  • The Kasion version number (found on the Settings screen inside the app).

  • Screenshots or screen recordings, if applicable.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Kasion require an account?

No. Kasion works fully offline without an account, login, email, or phone number. All your data is stored on your device.

Where is my data stored?

All transactions, budgets, assets, and receipt images are stored locally on your iPhone. We do not upload anything to our servers.

Is Kasion free?

Kasion is free to download and use. There are no subscriptions, ads, or in-app purchases.

Can I back up or transfer my data?

Your data is included in your standard iPhone or iCloud backup (depending on your settings). You can also export all transactions to CSV from the Analytics screen.

How do I delete all my data?

Deleting the Kasion app from your iPhone permanently removes all data stored by the app. There is no cloud copy to remove.

How do I contact support?

Email us at becipora79@gmail.com. We typically respond within one business day.
