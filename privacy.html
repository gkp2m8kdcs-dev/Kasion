Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Kasion is built around a simple promise: your financial data stays on your device. We don't collect it, we don't upload it, and we don't share it with anyone.

1. Data We Do Not Collect

Kasion does not require an account, login, email, phone number, or any personal identifier to use. The app does not collect, transmit, or sync any of the following:

  • Financial transactions, asset holdings, or balances
  • Receipt or attachment photos
  • Budget settings or category data
  • Usage analytics, device identifiers, or advertising IDs
  • Location data, contacts, or any other personal information

2. Where Your Data Lives

All data you enter into Kasion — including transactions, budgets, assets, net worth snapshots, and receipt photos — is stored locally on your iPhone using iOS's standard on-device storage mechanisms. We never have access to this data and there is no server component that stores it.

3. Permissions We Request

To deliver features you opt into, Kasion may request the following iOS permissions. Granting any of these is entirely your choice and can be revoked at any time in iOS Settings:

  • Camera — only when you tap the camera icon to scan a receipt for a transaction.
  • Photo Library — only when you pick an existing receipt image to attach to a transaction.
  • Notifications — only if you enable budget reminders or spending alerts.

Images you capture or pick are stored locally and never leave your device.

4. Third-Party Services

Kasion does not include any third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, crash reporting tools, or marketing trackers. The app is fully self-contained.

5. Children

Kasion is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect any information from children, because we do not collect any information from anyone.

6. Data Deletion

Because all data lives on your device, deleting the app from your iPhone permanently removes all stored Kasion data. There is no cloud copy to delete.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this Privacy Policy, we will post the revised version on this page and update the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will, where required by applicable law, be communicated through the App Store listing or an in-app notice.
